# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

This project aims to provide a respectful, professional environment for all contributors.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus on technical issues, not individuals.
- Accept feedback and collaborate in good faith.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, hate speech, or personal attacks.
- Doxxing or sharing private information.
- Deliberate disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or block participation for violations.
